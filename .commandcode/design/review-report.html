// GUT REACTION

Within ten seconds of a turn this feels like a well-behaved CLI: quiet, fast, no ceremony, no mascot, no gradient. The tool echo with its ❯ spine and the dim output under it are the best idea on the surface — you can always see what the machine actually ran, in order.

At the same time, nothing on screen says what this is, the one interaction a new user needs first (/) is invisible until discovered, and the first thing written after your prompt is the model thinking out loud in unlabeled dim text.
